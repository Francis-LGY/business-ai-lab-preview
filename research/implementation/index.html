What turns a promising prototype into useful organizational practice?怎样让有潜力的原型转化为有效的组织实践？

  • Frame important business questions as testable hypotheses.将重要商业问题转化为可检验假设。
  • Build bounded prototypes with explicit human review.构建具有明确人工复核环节的有界原型。
  • Evaluate outcomes and limits before scaling.在扩大应用前评估结果与局限。
