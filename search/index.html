Research directions研究方向 ↗

Four core directions, supported by shared methods and focused research themes.四个核心研究方向，以及相互支撑的研究方法与专题。

Human–AI Collaboration & Multi-agent Innovation组织人机协作与多智能体创新 ↗

Study talent development, knowledge transfer, cognitive workload, and the organizational consequences of generative AI and multi-agent systems.探索生成式 AI 如何支持人才培养、知识传承与认知负荷分配，以及多智能体系统如何影响组织与战略。

Intelligent Marketing & Platform Disclosure智能营销与平台信息披露 ↗

Connect digital consumer journeys, demand discovery, and lifetime value with research on AI disclosure, trust, and platform incentives.结合数字消费轨迹、需求挖掘与生命周期价值预测，研究 AI 信息披露、消费者信任与平台激励机制。

Smart Finance & Alternative Data智慧金融与另类数据价值发掘 ↗

Investigate large-model methods, quantitative strategies, and alternative data for financial prediction and interactive investment support.探索大模型、量化策略与另类数据在金融预测和交互式智能投资支持中的应用价值。

Smart Transportation & Public Infrastructure智慧交通与公共基础设施效能 ↗

Study infrastructure planning, conversational traffic control, dynamic routing, and incentives that improve system efficiency and worker outcomes.研究基础设施规划、对话式交通管控、动态路径规划，以及提升系统效率与劳动者福祉的激励机制。

Research Methods & Talent Development研究方法与人才培养 ↗

Connect causal inference and design science with AI prototyping, business analytics education, and doctoral research.连接因果推断、设计科学与 AI 原型，支持商业分析教育与博士研究。

AI Disclosure & Platform GovernanceAI 披露与平台治理 ↗

A focus within the marketing and platform-disclosure direction, examining transparency, trust, and incentive design.智能营销与平台信息披露方向下的专题，关注透明度、信任与激励设计。

Nano cases微案例 ↗

Twelve fictional business situations, each unpacked into a question, intervention, and evaluation.12 个虚构商业情境，逐一展开问题、干预与评估。

Aster Services: an AI knowledge desk星屿服务：AI 知识服务台 ↗

A fictional business scenario about support staff repeatedly search disconnected manuals, using a source-linked assistant with an explicit escalation path.一个关于客服人员反复检索分散的业务手册的虚构商业情境，拟采用带来源引用和明确升级机制的知识助手。

Northline Retail: from signals to stock decisions北线零售：从信号到备货决策 ↗

A fictional business scenario about store teams reconcile inconsistent demand signals, using a joint forecast review with human overrides.一个关于门店团队需要协调不一致的需求信号的虚构商业情境，拟采用允许人工修正的预测复核流程。

Harbor Works: redesigning factory handoffs港湾制造：重新设计工序交接 ↗

A fictional business scenario about production teams lose context between shifts, using structured handoff summaries reviewed by the next shift.一个关于生产团队在交接班时丢失关键上下文的虚构商业情境，拟采用由接班人员复核的结构化交接摘要。

Lumen Studio: making space for better review微光工作室：为更好的审阅留出空间 ↗

A fictional business scenario about rapid first drafts overwhelm reviewers, using quality gates and a slower final-review stage.一个关于快速产生的初稿让审阅者应接不暇的虚构商业情境，拟采用质量门槛与独立终审环节。

Cedar Supply: agents in procurement杉木供应：采购流程中的智能体 ↗

A fictional business scenario about buyers manually compare fragmented supplier quotes, using bounded comparison agents with human approval of orders.一个关于采购人员手工比较分散的供应商报价的虚构商业情境，拟采用具有边界的比价智能体和人工订单审批。

Meridian Learning: feedback that teaches子午学习：有助于学习的反馈 ↗

A fictional business scenario about learners accept polished answers without understanding them, using a guided feedback loop that asks learners to explain decisions.一个关于学习者接受流畅答案却没有真正理解的虚构商业情境，拟采用要求学习者解释决策的引导式反馈流程。

Pine Talent: questioning an automated shortlist青松人才：质疑自动筛选名单 ↗

A fictional business scenario about a screening team cannot explain ranking differences, using transparent criteria and a documented review channel.一个关于筛选团队无法解释候选人排序差异的虚构商业情境，拟采用透明标准与有记录的复核渠道。

Lake Analytics: knowing when to disagree湖畔分析：知道何时提出异议 ↗

A fictional business scenario about analysts defer to confident-looking model outputs, using confidence checks and independent challenge rounds.一个关于分析师过度依赖看似自信的模型输出的虚构商业情境，拟采用置信检查和独立质疑环节。

Orchid Care: preserving service autonomy兰亭服务：保留一线自主权 ↗

A fictional business scenario about frontline workers feel constrained by scripted recommendations, using discretion rules and recorded reasons for overrides.一个关于一线工作人员感到脚本化建议限制了自主性的虚构商业情境，拟采用自主裁量规则与人工修正理由记录。

Bridge Market: a small-business AI pilot桥市商贸：小企业的 AI 试点 ↗

A fictional business scenario about a small team has limited capacity to test new tools, using one narrow pilot with a clear stop rule.一个关于小团队缺少测试新工具的人力的虚构商业情境，拟采用一个具有明确停止条件的小范围试点。

Atlas Engineering: documenting expert judgment图谱工程：记录专家判断 ↗

A fictional business scenario about rare expertise is difficult to explain to new colleagues, using annotated decision examples rather than answer imitation.一个关于稀缺专业经验难以向新同事解释的虚构商业情境，拟采用带决策注释的示例，而非直接模仿答案。

Clearwater Data: privacy in everyday workflows清泉数据：日常流程中的隐私 ↗

A fictional business scenario about teams share more data than a task actually needs, using minimum-data prompts and local review checkpoints.一个关于团队共享了超出任务所需范围的数据的虚构商业情境，拟采用最小数据提示与本地复核检查点。
