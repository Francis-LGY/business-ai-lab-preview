SECTION 01

Project overview项目概况

RESEARCH PROGRAMME研究计划

Business Artificial Intelligence Lab at City University of Hong Kong (Dongguan) connects research, education, industry, and practical application. Drawing on the complementary strengths of the Hong Kong and Dongguan campuses, the lab brings generative AI and multi-agent systems together with causal inference and design science to address real business and societal questions.商业人工智能实验室位于香港城市大学（东莞），以“产学研用”深度融合为发展理念，发挥香港与东莞双校区的资源互补优势。实验室将生成式人工智能、多智能体协同系统与因果推断、设计科学等方法结合，围绕现实商业与社会问题开展跨学科研究、教学与应用探索。

SECTION 02

The central research question核心科学问题

Understand how mechanism design and AI-enabled technologies reshape organizational efficiency, marketing, financial decisions, and public infrastructure in complex business and social systems.理解机制设计与数智化技术如何在复杂商业和社会系统中重塑组织效率、市场营销、金融决策与公共基础设施管理。

SECTION 03

Four core research directions四个核心研究方向

CORE DIRECTION核心研究方向

Human–AI Collaboration & Multi-agent Innovation组织人机协作与多智能体创新 ↗

Study talent development, knowledge transfer, cognitive workload, and the organizational consequences of generative AI and multi-agent systems.探索生成式 AI 如何支持人才培养、知识传承与认知负荷分配，以及多智能体系统如何影响组织与战略。

Explore the programme查看研究计划 ↗
CORE DIRECTION核心研究方向

Intelligent Marketing & Platform Disclosure智能营销与平台信息披露 ↗

Connect digital consumer journeys, demand discovery, and lifetime value with research on AI disclosure, trust, and platform incentives.结合数字消费轨迹、需求挖掘与生命周期价值预测，研究 AI 信息披露、消费者信任与平台激励机制。

Explore the programme查看研究计划 ↗
CORE DIRECTION核心研究方向

Smart Finance & Alternative Data智慧金融与另类数据价值发掘 ↗

Investigate large-model methods, quantitative strategies, and alternative data for financial prediction and interactive investment support.探索大模型、量化策略与另类数据在金融预测和交互式智能投资支持中的应用价值。

Explore the programme查看研究计划 ↗
CORE DIRECTION核心研究方向

Smart Transportation & Public Infrastructure智慧交通与公共基础设施效能 ↗

Study infrastructure planning, conversational traffic control, dynamic routing, and incentives that improve system efficiency and worker outcomes.研究基础设施规划、对话式交通管控、动态路径规划，以及提升系统效率与劳动者福祉的激励机制。

SECTION 04

Research methods研究方法

Combine generative AI and multi-agent systems with causal inference, design science, and economic and management research.将生成式 AI、多智能体协同系统与因果推断、设计科学及经济管理研究方法结合。

Expected outputs & education预期产出与人才培养

  • Peer-reviewed research and conference exchange同行评议研究与学术会议交流
  • Conversational traffic control, interactive investment support, and multi-agent research prototypes对话式交通管控、交互式投资支持和多智能体研究原型

  • Practical projects for business analytics education and doctoral research商业分析教学实践与博士研究培养
