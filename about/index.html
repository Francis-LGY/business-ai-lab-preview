At Business Artificial Inteligence Lab, we explore how artificial intelligence changes the way businesses make decisions, organize work, and create value. Our proposed approach connects management research, computational methods, and human-centered inquiry to turn important business questions into testable ideas.Business Artificial Inteligence Lab 关注人工智能如何改变企业决策、组织协作与价值创造。我们拟将管理学研究、计算方法与以人为中心的视角结合，把重要的商业问题转化为可检验的研究想法，连接学术探索与商业实践。

Mission & approach使命与方法

Our illustrative programme combines experiments, field inquiry, computational prototypes, and critical reflection. The proposed aim is to understand where AI creates value and where organizational choices matter more than model capability.示例研究计划结合实验、实地研究、计算原型与批判性反思，理解 AI 在何处创造价值，以及组织选择何时比模型能力更关键。
