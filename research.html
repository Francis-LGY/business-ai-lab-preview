RESEARCH

Research directions研究方向

Four planned research directions connect methods with business and societal questions.四个拟开展的研究方向连接方法、商业与社会问题。

01

Human–AI Collaboration & Multi-agent Innovation组织人机协作与多智能体创新

Explore how generative AI supports talent development, knowledge transfer and cognitive workload, and how multi-agent systems can support research and strategy.探索生成式 AI 如何支持人才培养、知识传承与认知负荷分配，以及多智能体系统在研究构思和企业战略中的应用。

Questions to explore拟探索的问题

How does AI change peer pressure and career progression? How can tacit knowledge be made explicit?AI 如何影响同侪压力和晋升机制？如何促进隐性知识显性化？

These questions and applications are part of the proposed programme.上述问题及应用属于项目研究计划。

02

Intelligent Marketing & Platform Disclosure智能营销与平台信息披露

Study digital consumer journeys, demand discovery and lifetime value alongside disclosure, trust and platform incentives.结合数字消费轨迹、需求挖掘与生命周期价值预测，研究信息披露、消费者信任及平台激励机制。

Questions to explore拟探索的问题

How does AI disclosure affect trust? How can platforms design incentives for participants?AI 披露如何影响消费者信任？平台如何设计参与者的激励机制？

These questions and applications are part of the proposed programme.上述问题及应用属于项目研究计划。

03

Smart Finance & Alternative Data智慧金融与另类数据价值发掘

Investigate large models and alternative data for financial forecasting and interactive investment support.探索大模型与另类数据在金融预测和交互式智能投资支持中的应用。

Questions to explore拟探索的问题

What can hiring and venture-capital networks add to prediction? How can systems reflect investor preferences?企业招聘和风险投资网络能为预测提供什么增量信息？系统如何理解投资者需求？

These questions and applications are part of the proposed programme.上述问题及应用属于项目研究计划。

04

Smart Transportation & Public Infrastructure智慧交通与公共基础设施效能

Study infrastructure planning, conversational traffic control and dynamic routing.研究基础设施规划、对话式交通管控和动态路径规划。

Questions to explore拟探索的问题

How can transport facilities be planned and conversational systems support traffic management?如何规划交通设施？对话式系统如何支持交通管理？

These questions and applications are part of the proposed programme.上述问题及应用属于项目研究计划。
