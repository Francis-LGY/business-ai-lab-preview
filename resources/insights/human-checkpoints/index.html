SECTION 01

The question提出问题

This illustrative article asks: where should a human checkpoint sit?? A useful starting point is to describe the task, identify who makes the decision, and distinguish the model output from the result that matters to people.这篇模拟文章围绕“人工检查点应该放在哪里？”展开。起点是描述任务、明确决策者，并区分模型产出与对人有价值的实际结果。

SECTION 02

A practical lens实践视角

Consider a fictional team adopting an AI assistant. Faster first drafts may shift effort into review, coordination, and error recovery. The example invites us to follow the whole workflow rather than treating speed at one step as overall value.设想一个采用 AI 助手的虚构团队。初稿速度提升后，投入可能转向审阅、协作和错误恢复。这个示例提醒我们观察整个流程，而不是将单一步骤的速度等同于整体价值。

SECTION 03

A small experiment一个小实验

Choose one repeatable task. Record a baseline, define a quality threshold, and compare total effort including review. Ask an independent reviewer to assess the output. Stop if errors become harder to detect or if the review burden removes the expected benefit.选取一个可重复任务，记录基线、定义质量门槛，比较包含复核在内的总投入，并请独立审阅者评估产出。如果错误更难被发现，或复核负担抵消预期收益，则停止实验。

Limits of this example示例的边界
