SECTION 01

Abstract摘要

This fictional abstract outlines a framework for studying AI-agent workflows in knowledge-intensive business tasks. The example asks how task decomposition, human review, and recovery from errors could be evaluated together.模拟摘要：提出一个研究知识密集型商业任务中 AI 智能体工作流的框架，探讨如何共同评估任务拆分、人工复核和错误恢复。此文案不代表已完成的研究成果。

SECTION 02

Research design研究设计

  • Map tasks into agent actions and human review points将任务映射为智能体动作与人工复核节点
  • Compare workflow reliability under controlled failure cases在受控失败场景中比较流程可靠性
  • Measure quality, effort, and recoverability without claiming results考察质量、投入和可恢复性，不预设研究结果
