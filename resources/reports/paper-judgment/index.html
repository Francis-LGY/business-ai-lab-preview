SECTION 01

Abstract摘要

This fictional abstract proposes studying how managers combine AI recommendations with their own expertise. The illustrative design compares decision processes across tasks with different levels of uncertainty. No data have been collected and no findings are asserted.模拟摘要：本研究构想探讨管理者如何将 AI 建议与自身经验结合，比较不同不确定性任务中的决策过程。此处只展示研究设计示例，尚无真实数据或研究结论。

SECTION 02

Research design研究设计

  • Compare unaided, AI-assisted, and explanation-assisted decisions比较独立决策、AI 辅助决策与解释辅助决策
  • Examine calibration of confidence and willingness to challenge recommendations考察信心校准及质疑模型建议的意愿
  • Evaluate decision quality with pre-specified criteria使用预先设定的标准评估决策质量
