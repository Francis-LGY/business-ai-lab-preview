BAI Lab demo. (2026). Lumen Studio: making space for better review. Fictional case, not empirical research.

SECTION 01

Executive summary内容摘要

A fictional business scenario about rapid first drafts overwhelm reviewers, using quality gates and a slower final-review stage.一个关于快速产生的初稿让审阅者应接不暇的虚构商业情境，拟采用质量门槛与独立终审环节。

SECTION 02

Background & the business question背景与商业问题

In this fictional scenario, rapid first drafts overwhelm reviewers. The purpose of the example is to turn this business difficulty into a concrete question about AI, work, and organizational design. The organization and all circumstances are invented.在此虚构情境中，快速产生的初稿让审阅者应接不暇。本示例将这一业务困难转化为关于 AI、工作与组织设计的具体问题。案例机构及情境全部为模拟。

SECTION 03

An illustrative intervention示例干预设计

The fictional team tries quality gates and a slower final-review stage. Roles, review checkpoints, and exception routes are defined before the trial. A comparison workflow is kept so that apparent improvements can be examined rather than assumed.模拟团队尝试质量门槛与独立终审环节，在试点前定义角色、复核节点与异常升级路径，同时保留对照流程，以检验而非预设改善。

SECTION 04

Measurement & evaluation测量与评估

A possible evaluation would examine revision depth, originality, and reviewer workload. It would account for review time and recovery from errors, not just initial production speed. No measurements have actually been collected for this illustrative case.示例评估关注修改深度、原创性与审阅负担，同时计入审阅时间和错误恢复成本，而不仅是初始产出速度。此模拟案例没有实际采集数据。

SECTION 05

Illustrative outcome & limitations模拟结果与局限

In the invented narrative, clearer handoffs make some decisions easier to review, while unusual cases still require experienced judgment. This is a hypothetical outcome, not empirical evidence. The example cannot establish that AI caused an improvement.在虚构叙事中，更清晰的交接使部分决策更容易复核，但异常情况仍需要经验判断。这是模拟结果，不是实证证据，不能据此认定 AI 造成了改善。

SECTION 06

Discussion & takeaways讨论与启示

What would count as a worthwhile improvement in this scenario? Which tasks should remain with people? Use revision depth, originality, and reviewer workload as a starting point, identify a stop condition, and check whether benefits survive independent review.这一情境中，怎样才算值得继续的改善？哪些任务应由人负责？可以从修改深度、原创性与审阅负担出发，设定停止条件，并检查收益是否经得起独立复核。
