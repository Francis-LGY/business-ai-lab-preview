Biography个人简介

Dr. Chong (Alex) Wang is a Professor in the Department of Information Systems at the College of Business at the City University of Hong Kong. His research explores the impact of information technology on the generation, sharing, and utilization of information across various business and social contexts. His studies encompass a wide range of topics, including social media, online networks, the platform economy, financial technology, artificial intelligence, data governance, and digital privacy. His scholarly work advances the research frontier in these areas, addresses practical challenges faced by practitioners, and employs multiple methodologies to gather empirical evidence. His research has been published in leading academic journals such as Information Systems Research, MIS Quarterly, and Management Science. Professor Wang serves as associate editor for prestigious IS journals like MIS Quarterly, Information and Management, and Information Systems Journal. 

王翀是香港城市大学资讯系统学系教授、系主任。他研究信息技术如何影响商业和社会中的信息产生、分享与利用，涉及社交媒体、平台经济、金融科技、人工智能、数据治理和数字隐私。研究成果发表于 Information Systems Research、MIS Quarterly 和 Management Science 等期刊，并担任信息系统领域期刊副编辑。
