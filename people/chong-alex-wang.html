Biography个人简介

Chong Alex Wang is Professor and Head of the Department of Information Systems at City University of Hong Kong. He studies how information technologies shape the creation, exchange and use of information across business and society, including social media, platform markets, fintech, AI, data governance and privacy. His research has appeared in Information Systems Research, MIS Quarterly and Management Science, and he serves as an associate editor for leading information systems journals.王翀是香港城市大学资讯系统学系教授、系主任。他研究信息技术如何影响商业和社会中的信息产生、分享与利用，涉及社交媒体、平台经济、金融科技、人工智能、数据治理和数字隐私。研究成果发表于 Information Systems Research、MIS Quarterly 和 Management Science 等期刊，并担任信息系统领域期刊副编辑。

Research interests研究方向
