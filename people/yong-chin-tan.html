Yong Chin Tan陈勇锦

Associate Professor · Program Coordinator副教授 · 专业负责人

Biography个人简介

Yong Chin Tan researches marketing technologies and online retail consumer behaviour. He earned his PhD in Business (Marketing) from Singapore Management University and previously worked at Nielsen and Kantar Millward Brown. His work has appeared in the Journal of Marketing and Harvard Business Review.陈勇锦研究营销新技术及在线零售中的消费者行为，获新加坡管理大学商业（市场营销）博士学位，曾在 Nielsen 和 Kantar Millward Brown 从事市场研究。研究成果见于 Journal of Marketing 和 Harvard Business Review。

Research interests研究方向

Digital marketing, online retail, e-commerce and emerging marketing technologies.数字营销、在线零售、电子商务及营销新技术。

Education and affiliation教育与单位

PhD in Business (Marketing), Singapore Management University.新加坡管理大学商业（市场营销）博士。
