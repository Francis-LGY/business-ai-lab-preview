Junming Liu刘峻铭

Associate Professor · Associate Head副教授 · 副系主任

Biography个人简介

Junming Liu is Associate Professor and Associate Head of Information Systems at City University of Hong Kong. He received his doctorate from Rutgers Business School after studying physics at the University of Science and Technology of China. His work develops data-mining, network-analysis and optimization methods for big-data applications, including data-driven operations and human–AI collaboration.刘峻铭是香港城市大学资讯系统学系副教授、副系主任。他在中国科学技术大学学习物理，后获得罗格斯大学商学院博士学位。研究发展数据挖掘、网络分析和优化方法，应用于数据驱动运营及人机协作等领域。

Research interests研究方向

Data mining, network analytics, supply chain analytics, large-scale optimization and human–AI collaboration.数据挖掘、网络分析、供应链分析、大规模优化及人机协作。

Education and affiliation教育与单位

PhD in Information Technology, Rutgers Business School; BSc in Physics, University of Science and Technology of China.罗格斯大学商学院信息技术博士；中国科学技术大学物理学学士。
